ABOUT

About Shahziar

I am Shahziar Karim Safit, an Electrical & Electronic Engineering graduate from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET). My academic and research work focuses on wireless communication, physical-layer security, reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (RIS), 6G networks, antenna and RF engineering, machine learning, VLSI and semiconductor technology, and solar-cell technology.

My research experience includes analytical performance analysis of RIS- and relay-assisted wireless communication systems, selection combining (SC) and maximal ratio combining (MRC) diversity, secrecy capacity, secure outage performance, and different fading-channel models. I am particularly interested in deriving analytical expressions and using simulation-based performance evaluation to understand how system parameters influence reliable and secure wireless communication.

Alongside communication research, I have worked on machine-learning applications, RF/metasurface research, photovoltaic device simulation, and practical engineering projects involving embedded systems and IoT. I am interested in research-intensive environments where I can deepen my technical expertise, collaborate on meaningful problems, and contribute to innovative engineering solutions.
